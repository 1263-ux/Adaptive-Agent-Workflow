# Decision Log

## 2026-11-14 — v0.1 design baseline
- Lightweight, task-driven, outcome-oriented organization.
- Reuse mature domain Skills and methods instead of recreating them.
- Keep capability discovery, selection, mounting and authorization distinct.
- Make roles temporary responsibilities.
- Choose organization based on dependencies and net benefit.
- Keep host adapter thin and evidence-based.
- Use task-specific evaluation, not a universal quality score.
- Permit deletion and rollback of ineffective rules.

## Open decisions
- Minimal interoperable task state/version contract.
- Runtime capability probing and mounting semantics per host.
- Safe automated capability discovery and provenance checks.
- Evaluation asset interchange with open-agent-evals.
